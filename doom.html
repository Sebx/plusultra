Doom runs in WebAssembly with free Freedoom assets. The AI uses WebGPU when the UI shows “GPU active”; classic game rendering runs on the CPU. Doom's original aim assistance remains enabled.

Doom policy: a small linear head trained on 800 observations; validation 160/160 and held-out test 240/240, including wall recovery, dodging under fire, and closing to melee range to punch when out of ammo. Synthetic data with scope limited to this controller.

Controlled combat scenario: MAP01 with four added enemies, initial health and armor 100. This does not show that the model completes the level. Physical 4 GB mobile testing remains pending.
